The five formulas i

1
Investments / year = community × rate100 members at base rate = 10 new investments a year. Anchored: today we run ~3 per 100, capital-constrained.
2
Our stake = ownership % × ~€2M entry valuationToday ~2% (advisory + small syndicate tickets). With this round ~5-6%: the FFF and pre-seed tickets become affordable. The ladder mechanics are under the hood.
3
Stakes appreciate like our actual bookBase: 4x over 4 years. Our real portfolio stands at 4.0x with a capital-weighted age of ~3 years, so the base case is slower than what we have actually achieved.
4
Portfolio = our stakes + carry on others' moneyThe syndicate and instruments invest alongside us; Coventures earns ~20% of the gains on that external capital.
5
Engine = EBITDA × 3-6x, by growthA deliberately conservative consulting multiple. No platform premium on top: if anything, this understates.

Under the hood · every assumption, editable

Defaults are anchored to actuals (CRF cap table 6/2026) and to the fund instrument scenario. Edit any field; the model recomputes instantly. Reset by reloading.

Anchors (actual, June 2026): ~€2M services revenue (2025) · 100+ entrepreneurs in the network · 8 community companies, on 5 cap tables (~63%) · own capital ~€72k → ~€289k = 4.0x at ~3 years capital-weighted age (largely paper) · capital managed for others €119k → €384k (3.2x) · ~70% of syndicate capital is the community's own money.

Method in one line: community size × investment rate gives new investments; each stake is ownership % × entry valuation; stakes appreciate at the pace of our actual book (base 4x / 4 yrs); carry adds ~20% of the gains on co-invested external capital; the services engine is valued at a conservative EBITDA multiple. Paper values throughout; when and how cash returns is a separate conversation (see the deck's structure slide), deliberately not modelled here.
