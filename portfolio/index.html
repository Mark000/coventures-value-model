Service business holdings · own category, not in the totals

The venture portfolio above builds equity value toward exits, and its yardstick is the multiple on invested capital. Service businesses are a different animal: they build recurring cash flow, and the owner strategy is dividends rather than an exit.

That is why these holdings are never summed into the portfolio multiples. They appear here at full transparency: ownership, cost and owner strategy, carried at zero until there is a basis for a mark. When dividends start, they show up as cash events, not as paper value.

CRF is a tracking-stock investment company: each share series corresponds to exactly one portfolio company, and investors own series shares roughly 1:1 to the euros they put in. One series' return follows only its own company. Coventures Oy owns 30.2% of CRF and additionally holds venture stakes outside CRF: SpringDNA 25.00%, Elementic 0.87% and Arcticles 1.1% (the latter two at nominal cost). ValueLab (20.00%) is also held directly but belongs to the service business category, outside these totals.
